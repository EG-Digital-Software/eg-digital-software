Summary

Findings from the site check on 11 November 2026

The foundations of egeloma.com.au are good. Every page we checked has its own location-focused title and one clear heading, the sitemap lists all 49 pages, and the five country pages are linked to each other with hreflang tags. The two exceptions need fixing first: the website development page and an old About page tell Google that their main version lives on egdigital.com.au, so Google may leave them out of search results.

Clicks, rankings, backlinks and page speed will appear here once Search Console, keyword tracking and the other sources are entered.

EGEG Eloma SEO team
  1. Point the canonical tags on /website-development and /about back to egeloma.com.au, and permanently redirect /about, /services and /pricing to /company, /products and /plans.
  2. Expand the website development page to match the other service pages, with a Melbourne title and a full meta description.
  3. Connect Search Console and upload keyword positions so the next report shows clicks and rankings for Australia, New Zealand, the UK, the US and Canada.
