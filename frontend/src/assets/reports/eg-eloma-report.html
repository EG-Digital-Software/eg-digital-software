Monthly targets

Totals are for the date range selected on the dashboard. Copy each number from the platform's own insights for the same dates (Meta Business Suite, LinkedIn Page analytics, Threads Insights, Google Ads and Meta Ads Manager). Totals, changes, charts and goals recalculate automatically.

  1. Choose the date range on the dashboard first.
  2. Download a template (it already contains the current numbers for that range).
  3. Update the numbers in Excel or Google Sheets and save as CSV.
  4. Upload it here, check the numbers on the Enter numbers tab, then save.

Search Console and Analytics totals are for the date range selected on the dashboard: in Search Console use Performance, Search results, set the same dates, and copy the totals and the Countries tab. Backlinks come from your backlink tool (Ahrefs, Semrush or Moz). PageSpeed figures come from pagespeed.web.dev on the Mobile tab.

  1. Choose the date range on the dashboard first. Positions you upload are recorded for the last day of that range.
  2. Download the template. It lists all 20 target keywords for Australia with their target page.
  3. Fill in each position and the page that ranks, from your rank tracker or Search Console. Copy rows and change the country column (AU, NZ, UK, US, CA) to track other countries. Add new keywords as new rows.
  4. Save as CSV, upload it here, then click Save and refresh. Columns: keyword, country, position, url, search_volume.
